Do you work with clients worldwide?
Yes. Digital marketing work can be done online, so you can send an inquiry from anywhere and we can discuss your goals by message or email.
What does an SEO audit include?
An SEO audit reviews how well your website is set up to be found in search. It typically looks at on-page elements like titles and headings, keyword targeting, technical issues, and your local search presence, then lists what to fix first.
Do you manage Google Ads campaigns?
Yes. I offer Google Ads management, including Search, Display, YouTube and Shopping ads, as well as PPC campaign setup and optimization.
